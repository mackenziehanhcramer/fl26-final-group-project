##  Summary:
We were originally considering a general media recommendation system, and we decided to scope that broader idea into music specifically. We are imagining something along the lines of a radio where a user may move across stations that are actually made up of song recommendations from friends, etc. The main page will likely provide an interface where a user may choose a specific genre or theme for the music they are aiming to listen to.

## External API:
We are considering using Spotify's API: https://developer.spotify.com/documentation/web-api

Because we are hoping to use music, Spotify provides an interface with not only song information, but also additional metadata about artists, existing playlists, etc. Due to the rich information it holds, as well as the provided structure for existing playlists and ways to organize said music, we believe it would be a great fit for our project.
